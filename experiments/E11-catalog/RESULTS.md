# E11 — catalog saturation, and what novelty actually costs

**Run:** 2026-09-05 · `Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-Base` · v2 (decorrelated) binner · GLOBE_V2 mapper
**Two arms:** `out/` = `novelty=0.0` (**40 voices, complete**) · `out_nov0.75/` = `novelty=0.75` (**running**)

**Question:** how many distinct voices does this backend actually hold before new descriptions stop producing new voices?

---

## Why minting N voices is not the result

`GRAND-PLAN` §5 calls a few hundred audited voices "the minimum shippable thing". But a catalog of 500 voices where 300 are audibly the same voice is a catalog of 200 voices and a support problem. The number that matters is not how many were minted, it is how many are **distinct and still render correctly**.

Descriptions are generated by **inverting** the measure-first pipeline rather than hand-written — sampling points in bin space and captioning them with the same `caption_from_bins` used on real corpus audio. Hand-written descriptions cluster around whatever the author finds easy to imagine, and that bias would have shown up as fake saturation. 300 requested cells, 300 unique, all five bins occupied on every axis.

Every mint is audited by `service.mint` against three floors: **uniqueness ≥ 0.30**, **drift ≥ 0.40**, **consistency ≥ 0.43**.

---

## Arm 1 — `novelty=0.0` (the shipped default), 40 voices

| metric | mean | min | p05 | below floor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| drift | 0.457 | 0.354 | 0.363 | 4 / 40 |
| consistency | 0.564 | 0.454 | 0.464 | 0 / 40 |
| uniqueness | 0.616 | **0.149** | 0.294 | 3 / 40 |

**Vendi (normalised) 0.482 → 20.2 effectively-distinct voices from 42 minted.** Against `RESEARCH/06`'s anti-mode-collapse target of ≥0.35 that passes, but **fewer than half the voices minted are effectively distinct.**

### The catalog gets harder to add to as it fills

| voices so far | clean rate | mean uniqueness | mean attempts |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | 90% | 0.803 | 1.10 |
| 10–20 | 80% | 0.592 | 1.40 |
| 20–30 | 90% | 0.612 | 1.10 |
| 30–40 | **60%** | **0.457** | **1.70** |

**Mean uniqueness nearly halves over 40 voices (0.803 → 0.457) and retries rise from 1.10 to 1.70.** The nearest pair in the catalog sits at 0.149, half the floor — two voices the audit had to accept after exhausting its attempts.

This is saturation, at forty voices, against a target of "a few hundred". Extrapolating the trend is not safe from four blocks, but the direction is unambiguous and it appeared immediately.

Failure modes, by count: **9** uniqueness collisions, **8** drift-below-floor attempts (4 accepted after retries), **2** consistency failures.

## Arm 2 — `novelty=0.75`, preliminary (10 voices)

E12 found the mapper's generative branch had a defect capping it at 64 distinct outputs, and after fixing it, raising `novelty` cut geometric collisions dramatically. E12 was **geometry only** and said explicitly that a setting winning on geometry could still render badly. This arm is that test.

> ⚠️ **10 voices. Preliminary.** Recorded because the effect is large and one-directional, not because it is settled.

| | novelty 0.0 (n=40) | novelty 0.75 (n=10) |
|---|---|---|
| uniqueness mean | 0.616 | **0.861** |
| uniqueness min | 0.149 | **0.599** |
| uniqueness below floor | 3 | **0** |
| **drift mean** | **0.457** | **0.390** |
| **drift below floor** | **4 / 40 (10%)** | **5 / 10 (50%)** |
| consistency below floor | 0 / 40 | 1 / 10 |
| **clean rate** | **80%** | **20%** |

**Novelty buys uniqueness and pays for it in drift.** Collisions vanish entirely — minimum uniqueness rises 0.149 → 0.599 — while the share of voices failing the drift floor goes from 10% to 50%, and the clean rate collapses from 80% to 20%.

Drift is the vocoder round-trip: intended vector vs the vector re-extracted from the rendered audio. A falling drift means **the backend is not faithfully reproducing the identity it was handed** — the voice you asked for is not the voice that comes out.

**This is exactly what E12 could not see, and it vindicates E1's off-manifold warning** that E12 had put in doubt. The vectors are more distinct from each other *and* less renderable. Geometry improvements did not survive contact with the decoder.

---

## Where this leaves the catalog

Nothing here supports changing the default yet, and the two arms together frame the real problem:

- **`novelty=0.0` saturates** — uniqueness halves within 40 voices, and 3 pairs already breach the floor.
- **`novelty=0.75` does not saturate but does not render** — 50% below the drift floor at n=10.

So the capacity limit is **not** a knob that was set wrong. It sits between two failures, and the useful settings are in between. **Next: an arm at `novelty≈0.45`**, where E12 measured collisions already down (12.7% vs 19.0%) and the move from the anchors is half as far.

`gmm_components` is the other lever and E12 rates it higher than novelty — k=1 scored best on spread, collisions and plausibility simultaneously. It has had **no** rendering test at all, and given what this experiment just did to E12's geometry conclusion, it should get one before it is believed.

## Not established

- 40 voices in one arm, 10 in the other; a matched-n comparison at 40 is pending.
- One backend, one corpus, English only, one mapper configuration.
- **Nothing has been listened to.** Drift, consistency and uniqueness are all machine metrics against an ECAPA encoder. Whether these 40 voices *sound* distinct to a person is unmeasured and is the single highest-value unknown in the project.
- The saturation curve is four blocks of ten. It is a direction, not a fitted trend.

## Reproduce

```bash
# control arm (already in out/)
envs/qwen3/Scripts/python.exe experiments/E11-catalog/run_catalog.py --n 300

# a novelty arm
envs/qwen3/Scripts/python.exe experiments/E11-catalog/run_catalog.py \
    --n 300 --novelty 0.75 --out experiments/E11-catalog/out_nov0.75

# read either one, including mid-run (opens the DB read-only)
envs/qwen3/Scripts/python.exe experiments/E11-catalog/analyse.py --out <dir>
```

About 2.5 minutes per voice on a 6 GB RTX 3060. `analyse.py` works on a partial run and cannot disturb one.
