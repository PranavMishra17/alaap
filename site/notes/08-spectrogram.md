# 08 — spectrogram

**Lens.** The phonetics lab's Sona-Graph print: a voice as a shape burned onto graph paper, pitch traced over it in red pencil. Down a column: the same sentence, three voices. Across a row: one voice, any words.

**Palette.** Paper `#f1f2ee`, grid `#c9d5df`, ink `#1f1d1a`, scorch `#7a5c3e`, pencil `#c2372f`, graphite `#66645e`.

**Type.** Gentium Book Plus: the description in large italic, headings, every clip's words. Fira Sans body. Courier Prime for what the instrument prints: labels, roles, axis ticks, clip metadata, the rules. Tiro Devanagari Hindi on `lang="hi"`.

**Signature mechanic.** Every clip is drawn from its own audio in the browser: fetch, decode, windowed FFT on a log-frequency axis, pitch by autocorrelation. No images exist. The hero is the whole input and the print it produced, burned left to right on load. Choosing another description re-burns the print from that pre-rendered seed and plays it, a playhead crossing the paper; the page says nothing generates live. Row widths are proportional to duration: one time scale per section. Reduced motion skips the burn.

**Tried and removed.** The time stamp in the print's corner (duplicated the caption). The "not there yet" grid left a hole under item one: now two packed columns. Pacing labels overflowed at 390 px: they wrap.
