# 20 · hoarding

**Lens.** The hand-painted cinema hoarding: enamel on board, up on bamboo scaffold, the painters'
pencil grid showing through, the cast painted flat and huge. Panels alternate enamel (the painted
claims) and primer (the reading). The last panel is the unpainted end of the board, pencil only,
and that is where what is not there yet is written.

**Palette.** Peacock `#0F5E6B` (board), marigold `#F4A300`, rani `#E23A7A`, bone `#F3EAD3`
(primer, top strokes), soot `#161311`. Four enamels and the outline; no gradients.

**Type.** Modak for the painted title and two painted heads (fill, soot stroke, hard rani offset,
a small displacement). Teko for what a sign-painter letters in condensed caps: strap, credits,
buttons, terms. Martel for reading.

**Signature mechanic.** The voice, painted. Every clip is decoded and split into eighteen bands
of pitch; each band is a brush stroke, thick where loud, in rani, marigold or bone by height, with
a hard soot shadow like the lettering. At rest every clip is already painted; press play and its
strokes are laid down left to right in time with the sound. The board is painted in square by
square on arrival (not under reduced motion). Three painted figures, one per description.

**Tried and removed.** A gloss on the enamel. A fixed-height hero (dead field under the lede).
Keying clips by id (the hero repeats the mentor's seed; rows are elements now).

**Foregrounded.** §1 as the title panel with the example; §4 on the first primer; §8 as the cast
with billing and a five-frame scene strip; §5 with each caveat painted beside its claim; §7 as a
chain; §6 in full on the unpainted end; §11.
