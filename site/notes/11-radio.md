# 11 — radio

**Lens.** All India Radio and the radio drama: a Bakelite valve set with a lit tuning glass. The dial is the navigation: eight stations are the sections, the needle is the scroll position, and every clip plays through the set's one audio path and magic eye. Static marks the station with nothing on air.

**Palette.** Bakelite `#2E1912`, shadow `#452519`, glass `#F2DDA8`, unlit glass `#CDB37C`, ink `#241108`, brass `#C9A24A`, needle `#C8352A`, eye `#7FD9A6`.

**Type.** Martel 900/700/400 for wordmark, headings, body and Devanagari (`lang="hi"`); Khand 500/600 for dial, eyebrows, buttons and metadata. Both carry native Devanagari.

**Signature mechanic.** The tuning dial. Drag, click or arrow-key the band, or pick a station: the page scrolls and the needle follows. The glass names the tuned station, and while a clip plays, the speaker and exact words. "Switch on" lights the glass and plays the mentor's seed. Preset keys replay the three stored seed clips and say so; "Play the scene" runs the five lines back to back.

**Tried and removed.** Brass dots on the programme listing (the accessory). Smooth scrolling while tuning: the needle lagged the pointer; tuning is now instant. Station labels on phones: the glass names the station instead.

**Foregrounded.** §1 and §8 in the hero as preset keys, §5.1 and all eight clips as the play, §3 with the checked Hindi sentence, §4 as a programme listing, §7 as a signal chain with §5.8, §5.2–5.9 as the log, §6 in full over static, §11 as sign-off.
