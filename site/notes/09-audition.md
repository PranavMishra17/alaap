# 09 · audition

**Lens.** The casting room. The page is one casting file read top to bottom: the notice, the
breakdown, the sides, the conditions of the call, the routing slip, the casting director's notes,
"thank you". The three characters are the three roles; their seed clips are the actor's slate.

**Palette.** Photocopy paper `#EDEEE9` (cool, not cream), ink `#17171A`, ballpoint blue `#1E3F96`,
highlighter `#F7E64B`, pencil grey `#6F7069`, photo grey `#C9CAC4`, print white `#FAFAF7`.

**Type.** Khand 700 (Latin + Devanagari) for the notice and every heading; Courier Prime for
anything typed onto a form or a script page (descriptions, slates, the sides); Hind for body,
including the Hindi line. Both ITF faces, so the Indic-first feel is in the letterforms, not decor.

**Signature.** The headshot with no face: an 8×10 print whose portrait is the slate clip's own
loudness, decoded with Web Audio, drawn top to bottom and mirrored so it reads as a silhouette.
No face, because no recording of anyone was ever taken.

**Mechanic.** The callback. Two ballpoint buttons queue clips back to back: the mentor's slate then
both lines (one voice, three sentences), and mentor then scout (two voices). While a clip plays a
playhead sweeps the portrait, and on the sides the words are swept with highlighter, which stays
once a line has been heard, like an actor's marked script. Nothing generates live; the page says so.

**Tried and removed.** A typed-description input (nothing should look like it generates). A page
number on the sides (a digit not in the allowlist). A highlighter rule beside the clip caveats (the
yellow must mean "heard", nothing else). Heading widths that forced ugly widows.

**Foregrounded.** §1 as the notice form, §8 as slates and sides, §4 as conditions of the call,
§7 as a routing slip, §5 and §6 side by side as ticked and unticked notes.
