# 21 · matchbox

**Lens.** One uncut press sheet of Indian matchbox labels, flat two-plate litho. Every clip is a
label: frame, brand band, a figure drawn from what the voice says (mentor a mountain, scout smoke
on a ridge, innkeeper a pot on a fire), the description as slogan, the exact words, a duration
corner, a striking strip. The master आलाप label is a rising sun. §6 is eight blank, unprinted
positions.

**Palette.** Board `#E7E0CA` · Key `#241B13` · Viridian `#1E6A4A` · Rani `#C93B78` (second plate,
always 1.6 px off-register; `#A32A60` under small text) · Strip `#5C4033` · Ember `#F08A24`.

**Type.** Jaini (Latin + Devanagari) for brand names, आलाप, headings; Martel body; Pragati Narrow
for statutory lines, corners, buttons.

**Signature mechanic.** The strike. Each strip carries the clip's real loudness envelope, decoded in
the browser, as a pale trace. Striking plays the clip and burns it in: soot, a scratch through it,
an ember at the head; struck labels stay struck. Sequences strike the scene in order or the
mentor's three. The sticky colour-control bar is the contents. Reduced motion: no ember, the mark
advances in steps.

**Tried and removed.** Dashed row dividers. Sticky master label. A grid for Row F. "Drifting"
from the approved §4.2 wording, since §10 bans the stem. Rani text at label size (3.6:1).

**Foregrounded.** §1 as the master label, §8 as Rows B and C, §7 as the instruction strip, §4 as
Row E, §5 as Row F with proof notes, §6 as blank positions, §11 as the imprint.
