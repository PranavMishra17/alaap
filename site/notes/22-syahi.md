# 22 · syahi

**Lens.** The tabla head as one drawn object on paper: braided gajra, straps, chaat, maidan, and at the centre the syahi, the black paste of iron and rice that tunes the drum. Quiet type around it; the strip recites the page as bols (धा तुन धिं ना ता तिरकिट तिं के), the struck one where you are.

**Palette.** Paper `#e2ddd4` · skin `#efe9df` · ink `#1f1d1a` · syahi `#0b0b0a` · iron `#4b5561` (the one metal, from the syahi: labels, struck bol, spoke, focus) · leather `#4d372a` (gajra, straps).

**Type.** Martel for display and body, Latin and Devanagari together. Anek Devanagari at 80% width for utility: lengths, flags, buttons, figure labels.

**Signature mechanic.** The syahi is the real spectrogram drawn radially: fetch, decode, windowed FFT; time clockwise from the top, log-pitch outward. Polarity is the drum's: paste black where silent, worn pale where the voice is loud, so every clip wears a different syahi. Striking a row puts it on the head; the spoke sweeps, paste ahead intact, the voice wearing in behind. Bayan and dayan carry mentor and scout saying one sentence. Scripts off: a plain drawn head, native players.

**Tried and removed.** Dark-is-loud paste (a grey smear, not a syahi). The legend in the intro (now under the object). Three-column caveats. Hz ring labels.
