# 16 — parser

**Lens.** An interactive-fiction transcript, Infocom-style: rooms, `>` commands, replies, a
status line. The page is a game already played; the visitor takes over at the one live prompt.

**Palette.** Indigo `#171A45` ground · indigo-2 `#21245A` cards · ivory `#EFE8D6` text · ash
`#A6A4C4` caveats · marigold `#F4B942` prompt and labels.

**Type.** Rozha One for the title and room names (carries Devanagari too). Courier Prime for
everything typed or printed. Tiro Devanagari Hindi for the Hindi sentence.

**Signature mechanic.** A real parser, thirty-odd commands (`inventory`, `play scene`,
`examine mentor`, `compare mentor scout`, `describe a voice`). It can only replay the three
pre-rendered characters and says so in every reply. `describe a voice` matches your words to
the nearest and names it. The §6 limits are refusals: `say the line apologetically` →
"You can't do that yet." Now-playing lines are logged with ▶/■. The status line tracks the room
being read; Moves counts commands.

**Unfolding.** Reads whole with scripts off; the parser is a layer on top.

**Tried and removed.** A staggered fade-in on the hero lines (stock reveal). A 20rem dead right
margin on every room: the column is now centred and only the Ledger's caveats hang into the
margin when the screen has one.

**Foregrounded.** §1 example as the first exchange; §4 as room headings; §5 as Ledger entries
with caveats as marginalia; §6 as The Locked Door; §7 as the Workshop chain; §8 in the Voice
Room and Scene; §11 as Exits.
