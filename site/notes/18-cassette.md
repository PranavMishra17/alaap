**Palette.** Shelf `#2E4A43`, card `#EBE3CC`, label `#F4EFE3`, ink `#1A1D26`, red `#C4391F`, ballpoint `#234A80`, oxide `#4A2E1E`.

**Type.** Khand for display, panel tags and deck keys (condensed, Indian-foundry, sets the Devanagari title). Karma for body. Kalam for the handwritten label, the descriptions and the pencilled rule.

**Signature mechanic.** One tape, two sides. Side A is the three minted voices; the label rewrites itself in ballpoint with the current description. Turn over flips the cassette in 3D to side B, the five-line scene, and the label carries the current line. Play, Rewind, Forward and Stop drive the real clips; the reels redistribute tape by elapsed time and spin only while audio plays; a finished side asks to be turned over. The status window prints the exact words, the length and the emotion caveat where it applies. "Pre-recorded" is stated under the deck.
