# 03 — ragamala

**Lens.** A ragamala album. Painters were handed a verse describing a raga as a person and
painted the person; Alaap is handed a description and returns a voice. Every section is a
bordered leaf on lapis cloth with an orpiment Devanagari caption band.

**Palette.** lapis `#1F2E63` (cloth) · wasli `#DDBE7E` (leaf) · vermilion `#B5321C` (border,
mentor) · orpiment `#E9C24B` (bands, gold) · ink `#1B1510` · lead white `#F1E6CB`
(panel ground) · malachite `#2F6A50` (innkeeper).

**Type.** Rozha One for display and every Devanagari band, one face for both scripts; Martel
for body; Martel Sans for labels and ticks.

**Signature mechanic.** Every panel starts as an underdrawing. The hero paints itself when
seen, in four layers: ground, forms, figure, gold. Choosing a description's caption band paints
its figure the same way and plays its seed clip; the panel is haloed while it sounds. Cues chain
the pre-rendered clips; one plays the mentor's seed and two lines. Reduced motion paints
instantly; without scripts everything is painted and native players show.

**Removed.** Highlight arcs on the mentor's hills (read as birds). The rule under the last
scene line (doubled the caveat rule). The pacing scale's "measured" label from inside the bar,
hidden by the clean band. An orpiment focus ring, invisible on wasli; now ink.

**Foregrounded.** §1 example as the hero verse with its seed clip; §8 all eight clips; §4.1 via
the three-sentence cue; §5.5 the confirmed Hindi line as a caption band; §5.7 as a bounded
illustration; §6 in full; §7 as a procession.
