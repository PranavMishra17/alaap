# 13 · grimoire

**Lens.** A pothi, the stitched-leaf manuscript of the subcontinent, wrapped in indigo cloth:
twelve ruled leaves, name and subject in the left margin, a Devanagari folio in the right, red
rubrics, glosses in the outer column. A book of voices, one entry per leaf.

**Palette.** Neel `#212B47` (cloth ground), Leaf `#D6C08F` (page; `#B9A26C` rules), Ink
`#241E19` (`#5B4B3A` soft), Hingul `#9E2F24` (rubrics, frames, played strips), Hartal `#E9B949`
(orpiment: the portrait paint, and the pigment the scribe's corrections are marked in).

**Type.** Eczar, display: headings, the minting description, exact words, folio numerals, the
Hindi line. Martel, body. Martel Sans, utility: margins, captions, controls, caveat labels.

**Signature mechanic.** Every portrait is the clip's own waveform, decoded in the browser, drawn
head-down in a red frame as an underdrawing. Play, and it is painted in hartal in time with
the voice. On the mentor's leaf a different sentence repaints the same frame. "Recite the scene"
plays the five stored lines in order and paints the cast in turn. Reduced motion paints the
portrait whole at once; without scripts the portraits hide and native players show.

**Tried and removed.** A yellow wash behind the playing row: the inverted button and red words
already said it. The "nothing generates live" note moved under the hero player.

**Foregrounded.** §1 as the incipit, §8 as three bestiary leaves plus the scene, §5 with each
caveat as a marginal gloss, §6 as the scribe's corrections in orpiment, §7, §11 in the colophon.
