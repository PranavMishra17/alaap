# 04 — model-sheet

**Lens.** An animation studio's model sheet on a light table: Acme peg holes, a drafting title block, a lineup chart in construction lines (no faces), red pencil for the brief and every caveat. A model sheet fixes how a character is drawn; this one fixes how they sound. The page is a binder: model, rules, scene, measurements, pipeline, revisions.

**Palette.** Bond `#F4F6F3` · desk `#CBD3CE` · graphite `#2B2D2A` · smudge `#5F655F` · non-photo blue `#8CCBE3` (lines and waveforms, never text) · carmine `#C8322B` (Col-Erase red: briefs, caveats, playhead, revisions).

**Type.** Big Shoulders Display 800 for sheet titles; Kalam for the designer's hand and Devanagari (`lang="hi"`); Archivo body; Courier Prime for typed title-block fields and clip metadata.

**Signature mechanic.** The voice turnaround: Front / Three-quarter / Profile are three real sentences of one character, each drawn as the decoded waveform (OfflineAudioContext); "Turn" plays them in order with a carmine playhead. The brief is the button: choosing one writes the description onto the sheet (instant under reduced motion), rebuilds it, plays the seed, and says on screen that nothing generates live. Pacing is a bounded range whose readout is words only.

**Tried and removed.** A red underline on "sounds" in the thesis (the accessory). A "not approved" stamp on the revisions sheet: jokey beside plain limits. Sheet numbering: digits outside §9.

**Foregrounded.** §1, §2, §4 as "what the sheet fixes", §5.1–5.9, §6 in full as red-pencil revisions, §7 as a flow strip, all eight §8 clips.
