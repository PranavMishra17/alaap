# 12 · poster

**Lens.** A hand-painted Indian wall poster: one tall sheet on a slate wall, bands of flat enamel
with brush edges, a giant painted Devanagari title, the cast as the billing, and every caveat
pasted over its claim as a torn paper slip. The fine print is §6 in full.

**Palette.** Haldi `#F2B41E` (ground), Sindoor `#C6201A`, Neel `#1F3E92`, Kajal `#17140F`, Chuna
`#F4EEDD` (slips, fine print), Gulabi `#E4437F` (playback progress only). Wall `#2B2E36`.

**Type.** Yatra One (truck-art lettering, Latin and Devanagari) for title, band names, shout-lines,
cast names. Khand (condensed) for credits, labels, controls, fine print. Eczar for body. Painted
lettering: fill, black stroke, hard offset shadow in a second enamel, a small feTurbulence
displacement.

**Signature mechanic.** The paper slips: each result is painted big and its caveat pasted over it,
askew. The cast band is the live moment: tap a name, its panel lights yellow and the waveform,
drawn from the real clip, fills. "Play the scene" runs the five lines in order and scrolls to the
speaking line. The masthead pairs the §1 example with its real seed clip.

**Tried and removed.** A corner "not a product" ribbon (the fine print covers it). The
research-pass date in the topline. Slips beside scene rows (they covered waveforms). Sindoor
darkened from `#D8261E` so cream body text clears AA.

**Foregrounded.** §1, §8 (cast and scene), §4 as top billing, §5 as the measured ledger with
slips, §7 as the credits diagram, §6 as fine print, §11.
