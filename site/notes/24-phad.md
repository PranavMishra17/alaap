# 24 · phad

**Lens.** The Rajasthani phad: one story on one painted cloth, read at night by the lamp carried
along it. The hero is the cloth: ten panels in strict order (title, three births, five lines, the
unpainted end), flat profile figures facing each other, never the viewer. The telling hangs below
on cotton; under 900px the cloth is hung tall.

**Palette.** raat `#1B130E` (night, outlines) · hingul `#A82D18` (panel ground) · harda `#E3A83A`
(lettering, kangura borders, the scout) · hara `#3F6B3A` (innkeeper) · asmani `#2F5B86` (mentor) ·
kapda `#EBDAB5` (cotton). One colour per figure everywhere.

**Type.** Rhodium Libre for display and all Devanagari; Karma body; Mukta for controls and captions.

**Signature mechanic.** The lamp lights one panel; the rest sit in the night. "Play the scene"
walks it along the line panels with the real clips; prose buttons send it back to the births.
Scrolling the cloth carries the lamp yourself, unless a clip is playing. Its glow breathes with the
AnalyserNode RMS. Each panel's hem is that clip's real loudness envelope woven as kangura
triangles, turning to cotton as the playhead passes.

**Tried and removed.** A `?autoplay` flag (headless refuses even muted play; the `-playing` shot
is a real Playwright click). Devanagari panel numbers. A rail explainer the title panel covers.
