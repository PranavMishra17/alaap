# 19 · tanpura

**Lens.** The tanpura, the drone under every alaap, laid down. Four strings are the header; each
string is one movement of the page, named as an alaap's movements are: alaap (slow, without
pulse), jor (the pulse enters), jhala (fast), bandish (the composition, not begun: what is not
there yet).

**Palette.** Chandni `#e3e7e5` (ground) · kohl `#1c2126` (ink, the sounding string) · chandi
`#a3adb0` (strings at rest, rules) · jiva `#3a6b73` (thread, pitch line, links). Cool, one accent.

**Type.** Halant, light, for display and all Devanagari: thin high-contrast strokes like wire.
Mukta for body and labels. Two families.

**Signature mechanic.** The strings. The jiva thread slides along the sounding string as you
read and a peg lights for the movement you are in. When a clip plays, its movement's string is
struck and carries the real waveform from an AnalyserNode, the other three resonate faintly, and
it rings down after the clip ends. Reduced motion: no vibration. One image: the example's seed
clip drawn as its spectrum, cool ink, no grid, pitch in jiva.
