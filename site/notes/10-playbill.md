# 10 · playbill

**Lens.** A theatre programme held in the hand at a play: a card cover on a velvet house
curtain, opened to spreads of laid paper with a gutter, an interval, a director's note and two
tickets at the box office.

**Palette.** House velvet `#3a0e14`, pleat `#52161f`, card stock `#e6a03a`, laid paper
`#edede8`, ink `#1c1a17`, grey ink `#5b574f`, spot red `#8e1f2c`.

**Type.** Rozha One for the title and every heading; Libre Caslon Text for body; Antonio for
cast names, labels, durations and buttons; Tiro Devanagari Hindi for
आलाप and the Hindi line.

**Signature.** The closed cover. The page loads as one stacked card and opens on its spine in
3D by button, tap or scroll; the inside cover and the cast page are underneath. Reduced motion
and no-script get the flat layout.

**Mechanic.** The follow spot. One clip at a time; the speaking row gets a pool of card-orange
light and its name turns spot red. Queue buttons play the scene from the top, one character
across three sentences, and two characters from two descriptions, replaying the pre-rendered
clips and saying so.

**Tried and removed.** A fleuron on the cover. A boxes-and-arrows flow (now a running-order
rail). A follow spot with hard edges. A cast page visible under the closed cover on phones.

**Foregrounded.** §1 cover and inside cover; §8 dramatis personae and the scene; §7 backstage
and running order; §4 and §5 after the interval; §6 the director's note; §11 tickets.
