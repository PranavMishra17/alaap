# 15 — kathputli

**Lens.** A shadow-puppet screen: leather figures on lamp-lit cloth, hung on kathputli
strings. The page is the cloth with one lamp fixed behind it; a described character is a
figure with no recording, given a voice.

**Palette.** cloth `#E8D3A0` (ground) · lamp `#FFEDB8` (glow only) · soot `#1E1611` (ink,
strings, buttons) · indigo `#2B3A67` (mentor) · madder `#A8321F` (scout, caveat marks) · leaf
`#3F6B3B` (innkeeper). Figures multiply-blend like dyed leather.

**Type.** Eczar 800/600 for display and the Devanagari wordmark; Yrsa for body; Martel Sans
for labels and durations.

**Signature mechanic.** Pick a description pinned above the screen: its figure is pulled to
the cloth (sharp, opaque) while the other two recede (blurred, larger, paler) and its seed
clip plays. The string from pin to head is redrawn every frame as the real waveform from an
AnalyserNode; the lamp flickers with RMS. Every player on the page hangs its words on a string
that vibrates the same way; sequences chain the pre-rendered clips. At rest nothing moves: a
figure is inert until given a voice.

**Tried and removed.** Drop-lines above the how-it-works string; a puppeteer's hand
silhouette; row borders under the players; evenly spaced pacing ticks; an idle sway on the
figures (contradicted the thesis and detached the strings).

**Foregrounded.** §1 example as the first pin; §8 all eight clips; §4 with two back-to-back
sequences; §5.7 as a bounded range with the clean zone; §6 in full; §7 as one string from
description to audio.
