# 17 · dubbing

**Lens.** The ADR booth's paperwork: cue sheet with lengths and takes, cast sheet with voice
tests, two-language script page, booth notes, timing to picture, session report, signal chain,
pink pages for the gaps.

**Palette.** Ledger stock `#E3E6DD`, typewriter ink `#16201E`, acoustic foam `#2A3734`, recording
lamp `#C9302A`, pencil `#56635E`, pink pages `#EDCBC6`.

**Type.** Big Shoulders Display 900 for headings, the cue sheet's poster face; Courier Prime for
everything typed; Tiro Devanagari Hindi for आलाप and the checked line, `lang="hi"`.

**Signature.** The loop: a rythmo-style band of the five-line scene, each cue as wide as its real
length, each waveform the actual clip decoded live. Press Play the loop: three cue blips, the lamp
goes on, a red streamer sweeps the band, and the takes play in order.

**Mechanic.** Controls say what they do: a voice test replays the stored seed clip and says so;
timing to picture is a bounded slider that stretches a drawn take, its end landing on marks placed
at the measured speeds. Nothing is re-timed or generated.

**Tried and removed.** A blinking lamp (a recording lamp is steady). "Check passed" stamped on the
English translation: the check was on the Hindi audio; both stamps now sit there. A six-line hero
that pushed the booth below the fold.

**Foregrounded.** §8 as cue sheet, §1 as cast sheet and thesis, §5.5 and §6.7 as script page, §4
as booth notes, §5.7 as timing, §5 as report, §7 as chain, §6 as pink pages.
