# 26 · poster · craft

**Lens.** Variant 12's hand-painted wall poster, re-cut by the craftsman: same enamel, lettering
and slips; the work is in the edges, spacing and type scale.

**Palette.** The poster's five enamels, a dark wall. Contrast: kajal/haldi 9.9, neel/haldi 5.2,
chuna/neel 8.4, gloss `#D9D3C3`/kajal 12.3, `#B9B2A3`/kajal 8.7, `#3D382E`/chuna 10.1.

**Type.** Yatra One (titles, names), Khand 500–700 (labels, controls, boxes), Eczar 400/500
(body; Devanagari at 1.3rem/1.6). Fallbacks metric-matched with `size-adjust`. CLS 0.011.

**Signature mechanic.** The programme strip: a stencilled kajal band that sticks, follows you, and
turns the sheet over (Landing · Technical map).

**Round 2.** Hindi plays (four voices × three lines, `indicCaveat` beside every player, a 404
keeps the words); the map is the other side of the sheet (`:target` with scripts off).

**Update pass (27's gulabi-on-kajal stage, nothing else).** Cast and scene share one kajal
ground. Each voice carries an enamel on its card, name, bars, lit bars, playing tint, progress
block and Hindi twin: mentor/guru haldi, scout/student gulabi, innkeeper/narrator chuna, elder
sindoor. The map takes the same logic with restraint: learned = sindoor fill, frozen = neel
shadow and chip, gate = kajal stamp with a haldi chip, key/done = haldi, proposal = gulabi
dashed; the map-side strip chip is gulabi. New pairs: kajal/gulabi 4.73 (text ≥ .85rem),
chuna/sindoor 5.0, chuna/kajal 15.9, chuna/neel 8.4; sindoor/kajal 3.17 only at display size
(elder's small text is haldi); chuna/gulabi 3.35 is bars and shadows, never text. Not coloured:
plain nodes, arrows, dropped nodes, slips. OG unchanged.
