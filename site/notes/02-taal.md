# 02 — taal

**Lens.** A Bhatkhande notation sheet laid on teentaal: one avartan, sixteen matras, four vibhags. Sam holds the name, scene, audience and mechanism; tali the four differentiators, one per beat; khali, on slate, what is not there yet; the last tali what is measured, caveat in reach; then back to sam.

**Palette.** Paper `#ecebe3` · Ink `#17150f` · Brass `#a9801f` (struck marks, current beat) · Brass-deep `#7e5c10` (small labels on paper) · Slate `#2a3431` (khali) · Chalk `#e4e6df` (on slate).

**Type.** Rozha One for display and every Devanagari bol; Hind for body; Rajdhani for utility: beat numbers, eyebrows, lengths, buttons, figure labels.

**Signature mechanic.** The sticky theka strip is the contents and follows the reader beat by beat, back to sam at the foot. Every clip is decoded in the browser and notated as sixteen matras: height is measured pitch, width is loudness, a dash is unpitched sound. Playing a row strikes matras in brass as the cursor passes; sequence buttons play the scene or the mentor's three sentences. Reduced motion steps one matra at a time.

**Tried and removed.** Brass labels on slate (3.4:1, now chalk). "Written, in Hindi or English" in the cycle figure (unsourced; now "in ordinary language"). Beat 16 at the page foot (now sam). Single-column tali and khali beats (now two by two).
