# 07 — console

**Lens.** The recording studio as a mixing desk. The page is a session: a tape-labelled console
on top, paper artefacts (script, session log) below, screened panel legends between. Alaap is
the level check: the seed clip before the take.

**Palette.** Console slate `#3B4249` (body), raised panel `#4A525A`, desk well `#2C3136`,
meter-face and paper ivory `#EEE5CC`, ink `#17181A`, meter-red `#C23A1E` (overload zone, hard
stops, the "but" in the log), lamp amber `#E0A63A` (live channel, pressed button).

**Type.** Kalam 400/700 for marker-on-tape (headline, character names, आलाप, the Hindi line;
real Devanagari). Archivo variable: body at normal width, panel legends at 75% width, caps,
tracked. Courier Prime for the script page, readout window and log.

**Signature mechanic.** Three channel strips, one per character, each with a working VU meter,
a real fader (Web Audio gain) and Level check / Solo buttons. "Play the scene" runs the five
lines through the desk in order; the speaking channel's lamp lights, its needle follows the
actual audio, and the readout and script page show the exact words.

**Tried and removed.** Spinning tape reels (the one accessory); a single long headline tape
(it orphaned "Keep it.", now its own scrap); auto-centred paragraphs (panels share one left
edge with the tape).

**Foregrounded.** §1, §4 as engraved legends, §5.7 as the varispeed fader with hard stops,
§5.8 as Invent / Find, §6 in full as empty take boxes on the log, §7 as the signal path, all
eight §8 clips.
